## 1. Primary Regulatory References

1. **AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024-2027):** Statutory norms governing approval of technical institutions, sanctioned intake calculations, supernumerary quotas, and penalty schedules.
2. **Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry (APAAR) Guidelines (Ministry of Education, GoI):** Standard operating procedures for lifelong student identification and EduLocker integration.
3. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023:** Legal framework governing student consent, data fiduciaries, purpose limitation, and data subject rights.
4. **National Credit Framework (NCrF) & Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) Guidelines (UGC):** Guidelines for academic credit accumulation and verifiable credentials.
