## 1. Statutory Alignment: DPDP Act 2023

AIRE is architected in full alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:

- **Purpose Limitation:** Student data is processed exclusively for technical education admission verification and statutory quota reconciliation.
- **Data Fiduciary & Processor Boundaries:** AICTE acts as the Data Fiduciary; institutions act as contributing fiduciaries; AIRE operates as the technical data processing platform.
- **Notice & Consent:** Student data is submitted under statutory educational mandates, with explicit consent collected at the time of institution admission for APAAR identity verification.

---

## 2. Human Authority Over Final Regulatory Decisions

- AI and automated scripts are strictly classified as **investigative and analytical assistance tools**.
- No automated process can issue a derecognition order, impose a financial penalty, or revoke an institutional approval.
- Final regulatory sanctions require formal committee review, recorded minutes, and dual-authorization approval by designated AICTE officers.
