- `CONFIRMED`: Explicitly extracted from `PROJECT_PLAN.md` and approved as baseline scope.
- `OPEN`: Requirement or policy parameter requiring formal product owner or regulatory confirmation.
- `NEEDS VERIFICATION`: Technical requirement awaiting empirical test verification or provider access.
