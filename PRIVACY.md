# Privacy by Design

AIRE enforces privacy principles in accordance with DPDP regulations:

1. **Purpose Limitation**: Data is processed solely for authorised admission reconciliation.
2. **Data Minimisation**: Sensitive identifiers are replaced with pseudonymous surrogate keys and cryptographic hashes.
3. **No Plaintext Logging**: Identifiers and sensitive payloads are strictly excluded from application logs.
4. **Audit Trail**: All accesses to identity vault records produce append-only audit events.
